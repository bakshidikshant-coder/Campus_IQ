Reporting notes

How metrics are calculated

Placement rate = students with status “Placed” ÷ eligible students × 100. “Not Eligible” students are excluded from the denominator. A student may have multiple history entries, but is counted once in placed-student totals. CSV files open in Excel and most spreadsheet tools.
